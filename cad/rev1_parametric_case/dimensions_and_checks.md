# Dimensions and mandatory checks

Current targets: 360 × 280 mm case footprint; 88 mm base height; 34 mm lid; 270 × 170 mm nominal screen opening; 320 × 240 × 3 mm mounting plate; generic 3.5-inch-drive tray.

These are project targets, not verified component measurements.

Before manufacturing:
- Measure the exact display module, including bezel, PCB, cable exits, and mounting holes.
- Measure the exact computer board, connectors, heatsink, and cable bend radii.
- Confirm drive form factor and mounting pattern; many drives do not share the assumed dimensions.
- Confirm hinge/latch strategy, lid retention, and fastener lengths.
- Check print orientation, tolerances, wall thickness, ventilation, and structural strength.
- Confirm all mounting holes align with the actual selected hardware.
- Test a small fit-check prototype before printing the full enclosure.
